Earth is a moving techno planet empire that is colonizing other planets and attaching them to Earth's outer shell through self constructing bridges, where the planets either get harvested slowly for resources or are used for extra space.

The Earth Empire is primarily run by the corrupt and greedy Theresa corporation, but the corporation "Cat Corp" is fighting against Theresa, working to turn The Earth into a utopia for humans, animals, aliens, and any creature.
