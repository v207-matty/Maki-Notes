# Kumel
## Exposition

Establish Kumel : A helper on their planet, able to manipulate all of the elements while most other supers on the planet can only manipulate one element

## Rising Action

A shooting star passes and the planet starts to shake, and behind the shooting star is a giant ship that releases a Bakers dozen of Zonia Empire soldiers onto the planet

## Climax

The Zonia Empire soldiers cause destruction on Kumels planet

## Falling Action

Kumel begins to battle the soldiers, slowly ramping up how aggressive they use their powers

## Resolution

The Zonia Empire soldiers are defeated, though a warning comes with their defeat : "This is merely a delay of the inevitable, Zonia will get the Kesumel and all will bow before him"

# Kina

Establish Gixaka Watch : A galaxy watching patrol that is supported by the Anile themselves

## Rising Action

The Gixaka Watch notice The Zonia Empire is getting closer and closer to the Kesumel

Kina wants to gather a group of heros to assist the Gixaka Watch, his desires are vetoed however

The Gixaka Watch is becoming more and more lenient with bad actors

Kina looks into this

Kina discovers that Gixaka Watch has been taken over by Kupo

## Climax

Kina works toward stopping Kupo

Kina cant stop Kupo but instead weakens their forces

Kina escapes the Gixaka Watch and wanders
