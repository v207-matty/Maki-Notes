## Exposition

Establish Kina : A regular that trains to become stronger and stronger every day, and trains to posses powers, working for the Giz

Establish Gixaka Watch : A galaxy watching patrol that is supported by the Anile themselves

## Rising Action

The Gixaka Watch notice The Zonia Empire is getting closer and closer to the Kesumel

Kina wants to gather a group of heros to assist the Gixaka Watch in dealing with the Zonia Empire, but their desires are ignored and denied.

The Gixaka Watch is becoming more and more lenient with bad actors

Kina looks into this

Kina discovers that Gixaka Watch has been taken over by Kupo

## Climax

Kina works toward stopping Kupo

## Falling Action

Kina cant stop Kupo but instead weakens their forces

## Resolution

Kina escapes the Gixaka Watch and wanders the Teinom Realm (Mortal Realm)
