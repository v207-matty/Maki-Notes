## Exposition

On the planet Chabur, inside a giant forest late at night, Sinco jumps from tree to tree, at incredible speeds, his hands grabbing branches as they come close, his tail grabbing branches below and then swinging off it to the next branch, Sincos tail then begins to twitch right before the next branch Sinco grabs snaps off the tree, Sinco instantly grabbed another branch and held onto the one that snapped, Sinco swings lower down, waiting. Several animals walk by in groups or by themselves, fast animals, slow animals, large animals, small animals, several animals, then 2 slow and small but meaty animals walk by, and Sinco pounces right at them, stabbing one and grabbing the other with his tail and then grabbing the tail and holding it in the air, then once Sinco ensures the first animal is dead he stabs the other animal, straight through the heart, dropping the corpses Sinco raises his right hand up at a tree, shifting his hand to point towards the ground behind it, then his hand glows for a second and then a beam of energy comes out, piercing the ground, Sinco quickly swipes his hand to the ground on the right of the tree and stops his beam, cutting the tree and causing it to collapse, Sinco then beams the tree to create a couple of logs, Sinco grabs the logs, stabs both the animals on the same branch, and walks towards the edge of the forest, right by the ocean, setting up a campfire, chopping up the animals into bits, and warming them up with the fire.

Sincos tail then begins to twitch rapidly, trees start falling inside the forest, and a chain reaction of trees collapsing on one another causes Sinco to nearly get crushed by a giant tree, Sincos campfire and food are crushed, and Sinco is mad, Sinco runs into the forest, arriving at the near center in 8 seconds, but is interrupted by several machines cutting down the tree with the only notable detail being giant letters on the sides : "TAZ", there is smaller text but Sinco can't make it out before having to jump up onto the trees, but they're chopped down as they are in the machines way, Sinco jumps towards trees to the side, Sinco grabs some branches

- Sinco runs inside the forest
	- Sinco makes it to the center of the massive forest in 8 seconds with just running
	- Several machines with a "TAZ" label are cutting down trees
- Sinco jumps upward on top of a tree and swings upward
- Sinco grabs 2 branches and pounces at one of the machines
	- Sinco jabs the branches into the machines shell as hard as he can and the sticks break

	- Sinco lazers out 2 logs that will fit inside his hands

	- Sinco jabs the logs into the machines shell as hard as he can and it doesn't do anything

	- Sinco begins to turn around as he jumps off the tree

## Rising Action

- Sinco is grabbed mid air by the tail
	- A surge of pain goes through Sincos entire body
	- Sinco struggles to do anything
- Sinco is being held by the tail by a flying mechanical ship with an arm popping out of the bottom and the same "TAZ" label as the machines
- The ship opens from the top and out comes a regular human with black hair, a closed black trench coat and pants
	- The man introduces himself as "Tirok"
- Tirok asks if Sinco is one of the "Dirty Chabin" that keeps messing with his machines
	- Sinco denies this : "I ain't no Chabin! I am a Chabui! You're the Chabin! You're destroying my home!"
	- Tirok: "Your weird self and your home is in the way of the future, either get with the program or get lost."
- Sincos tail goes numb
- Tirok enters the ship
	- The ship lets go of Sinco
	- The ship retracts the arm into its bottom
- Sinco grabs onto a tree branch with his hand and swings towards the ship, landing on top of it
	- Sinco: "My parents always told me you regulars were assholes."
	- Sinco raises his free hand to charge an energy blast
- Sincos tail suddenly sends a wave of pain throughout Sincos body as it returns from numbness
- Tiroks ship releases another arm from the behind that grabs Sinco
- Sinco gets tossed to the ground with no trees to help him
- Sinco hits the ground
	- The full impact of the toss and fall spreads throughout his entire body
- Tirok speaks before leaving : "If you Chabeten weren't assholes or jerks most of the time with your differences, maybe WE wouldn't be assholes!"
	- Tirok flies away to the sky in his ship, watching the deforestation
- Sinco falls unconscious
- Sinco wakes up, hours later, mid day
- The forest has a clear pathway in the center of it from the city to the ocean, not a single tree in the way of it
- Sinco gets up
	- He can feel his tail again
	- He's no longer in pain
- Sinco looks toward the city and sees a factory with the "TAZ" label
	- Sinco runs towards the factory
