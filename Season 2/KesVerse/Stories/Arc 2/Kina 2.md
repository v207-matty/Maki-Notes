# Kina : The Second Protector

In the vacuum of space, Kina drives their escape pod towards planet Burvak, there is a target of the Zonia Empire there that Kina wants to see, if they're not dead.

Kina arrives on the planet Burvak, they exit it and notice several miles of destruction, if this planet had a hero on it, they're most likely dead, but suddenly Kina is attacked by a someone in a mech suit, Kina has trouble avoiding their attacks but slowly they start to calm and sense the energy of the attacker, reading their intent before they can act on it, it's difficult to do but Kina manages and dodges most of their attacks, then they begin shooting at Kina, so Kina decides to charge and fire an energy blast at the center of the mechs chest and at the palms where the blasters are, the mech begins to try and run away but Kina catches up and stops them, pushing them to the floor. "Who are you and why did you attack?", the attacker takes the head of the mech off, revealing a humanoid figure, "My...my name is Gego! I...I thought you were with Zonia. Sorry.", Kina grabs Gegos hand and lifts him back up so he can stand on his feet.

"I ain't with Zonia! I'm tryna go against him!", "If you're interested in joining me you're welcome to.", Kina looks over at their pod, then behind it, noticing a Zonia Empire ship, "I think we have the perfect transportation means anyway if we can get that working.", Gego looks at the ship, "Oh yeah I can get that working, I used to be apart of the Zonia Empire, and I doubt they changed their infrastructure that much.", Kina looks at Gego confusingly, then chuckles. "All right then Gego, let's go do this!"

Kina and Gego both get in the Zonia Empire ship and head to the already inputted coordinates, going who knows where.
