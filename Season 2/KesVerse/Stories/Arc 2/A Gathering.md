# A Gathering

Through the vacuum of space, Kina and Gego fly through, going where-ever their stolen Zonia Empire ship is taking them, in the mean time Kina has been training their Nei control, and Gego has been using whatever he can on the ship to fix his armor or to at least provide a substitute until this mission is complete, Kina grows and Gego works, as they are approaching a planet they notice another Zonia Empire ship going the same direction except slower, Gego notices and gets slightly worried, they're descending onto the planet when suddenly they are attacked by a giant ground pillar that shoots through the ship and then retracts, taking Gegos helmet with it, as the ship falls onto the planet, Kina notices a figure flying in the air with their palm pointed at the ground, then they crash onto the planet. Kina looks at the dashboard of the ship, "Planet : Musele", "Species : Keselins", "Danger Level : Medium", "Calculated Planet Average Nei : 168.65".

Kina and Gego crawl out of the crashed ship and are met by the entire population, staring down at them, as they fully leave the ship, hands in the air, the population erupts in rage, moving towards Kina and Gego, there are demands for the murder of Kina and Gego, accusations that the Zonia Empire knows of the prophecy and want to stop it, then a giant ice and rock wall forms separating the population from Kina and Gego, comes down is the same figure Kina saw earlier, they speak, "Who are you?", "Why are you here?", "Are you with Zonia?", "What do you know about the Kesumel?". Kina answers, "I am Kina and this is Gego, we're here because we took a Zonia Empire ship to try and find Zonias main ship, we are not with Zonia, we are against him...", "The only thing I know about the Kesumel is that with it, Zonia will become a god.", 

Kina and Gego have nothing, but then in the sky comes another Zonia Empire ship, everyone gets scared except for Kina, Gego and Kumel, they all get in battle stance, but when the ship lands it isn't a Zonia Empire soldier, as far as they know, the humanoid from the ship identifies himself as "Stone" and asked if they know his brother "Kuro", or... anyone from Zonias Empire, they deny this and Stone is about to leave but then Kina asks Stone if he would like to join them in battling The Zonia Empire, Stone agrees and Kumel asks about the Kesumel, Kina says they won't let Zonia get it, Kumel wishes to join, and The Protectors leave on Stones stolen Zonia ship, soaring the realm.
