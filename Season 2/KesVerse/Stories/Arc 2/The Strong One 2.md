# The Strong One : Power like never before

It's a peaceful day on Chacuric, not a single conflict foreseeable, but alas, peace cannot last, another Zonia Empire ship arrives on the planet, this time a small one compared to the last one, out comes 3 grunt soldiers but one soldier that looks familiar to Stone. The soldier identifies himself as Korn and requests that "Tenos" join The Zonia Empire, Stone asks about Tenos, Korn gets confused, "Brother have you forgotten your name!?", Stone speaks up, "My name is Stone, The Strong One, I've never gone by 'Tenos'!", Korn puts a device on his face, clicking a button, then a beep is emitted from it, Kuro gets mad, "The highest Nei I can detect on this planet besides you is 61 brother! The highest in a population is usually in the 100s! This backwater planets population defies the universal metrics!", Stone gets curious, "What's 'Nei'?", Kuro gets upset, "It means power! Have they not a lick of the Ige language on this planet? It gets worse and worse!", "Brother Tenos! You WILL join me in the Zonia Empire!", Stone gets in a fighting stance, "And if I don't?", Kuro sighs, "Then you will share the same fate as our home planet: Death".

Stone charges towards the ship, distracted by the guards shooting at him, so he flies through 2, about to go for the 3rd when Kuro steps into battle, stopping Stone with one hand, throwing Stone to the ground. "Your Nei is 130 brother, you stand little chance against me". Stone charges at Kuro, "I'll take that chance!". Stone lands a good jab on Kuro and goes on the backfoot, making Kuro pursue him, they both are flying across Chacuric, Kuro slowly getting closer and closer to Stone as he focuses, Stone has a technique he made that he has been wanting to try on someone. He takes the energy inside him, flows as much as he can or wants to a point or multiple in his body, he shrinks the size of it smaller and smaller inside him, it lowers his overall energy, making him less strong, but when he performs something that would need a lot of energy he stops keeping it small and lets it explode within him and flow if it's not used, but it spends enough time inside his points area that when he stops it's a massive power boost, relative to his base power anyway.

Stone performs this technique on Kuro, a massive explosion comes from it, and as the smoke settles, Kuro is just floating there, furious and only the tiniest bit physically affected, his hands bearing scratches from blocking. Stone blinks and Kuro is near his face, grabbing him by the neck. "YOU! YOU MADE ME THINK I WAS ABOUT TO DIE!" Kuro flies straight into the ground, Stone in front to take most of the damage as the ground crumbles under the force and a massive shockwave goes throughout the entire planet. "YOU TOOK THE NEI POINT COMPRESSION AND MADE ME THINK I WAS ABOUT TO DIE!!". Stone weakly asks "The Nei Point Compression?". Stone is slammed into the ground forcefully. "STOP ACTING DUMB! I KNOW YOU'VE BEEN OFF THIS STUPID PLANET!", Kuro throws Stone through a mountain, the debris falling onto a city with Stone. Kuro flies quickly afterward, grabbing Stone and dragging him to the Zonia ship hastily.

As they arrive at the ship, Kuro activates his face device again, it beeping once more. "Your Nei is at 58 brother, do not make me hurt you more before it's too late to heal you to at least 75 so you can function". Stone is too stunned by pain to say anything, Kuro and the grunt soldier walk into the ship, Stone being dragged by Kuro into it as well, being forced to stand and walk for himself, the grunt soldier starts the ship, and as the door begins to close, an axe flies through the grunt soldiers chest, killing them, Kuro then gets stabbed through by a sword. "This is what happens when you take chances, 'brother'." Stone says, tossing Kuros corpse through the ship door, the ship then speeds off with Stone inside, resting as the ship goes wherever.
