# The Strong One 2 : Power like never before

Another Zonia Empire ship arrives on Chacuric, this time a small one, out comes 3 grunt soldiers but one soldier that looks familiar to Stone

The soldier identifies himself as Korn, Stones brother, revealing their planet and bloodline are dead, them being the last 2 left, demanding that Stone join him

Stone isn't interested, zooms straight towards the ship but is stopped by the grunts, briefly gets into battle with the grunts, and kills 2 but right before the 3rd Korn steps in and single handedly sends Stone away, If Stone won't join Zonia then Stone has to die, same with this backwater planet, Stone doesn't want this to happen, Stone has been practicing something, he has a technique he created that could easily end this, but he needs time to charge it.

Stone he flies across the entire planet several times, Zorn and the grunt soldier following, Stone feels the energy inside him, compresses the energy, causing him to slow, moves the energy towards his hands, they glow, then he forms an energy blast, but right before releasing he uncompressed his energy in his hands, increasing his power from 130 to 185, firing at Korn and the grunt.
