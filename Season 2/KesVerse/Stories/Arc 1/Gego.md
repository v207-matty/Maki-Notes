# Gego

On the planet of Burvak there exists a man named "Gego" with the most advanced technology ever created on said planet, and yet he keeps it all to himself, this is so that he may be prepared for the arrival of more soldiers from The Zonia Empire on his planet, he was once apart of their ranks but due to a dispute there was a falling out and Gego impulsive at the time, causing major sabotages to The Zonia Empire systems right after the falling out, making them fall behind schedule by 5 years, and that was 13 years ago,

That day has arrived, it's been 2 years since they were last here, but now they are back, 8 Zonia Empire soldiers arrive on Burvak looking for Gego, causing destruction every where they go, sending the general population back technology wise by entire decades. Gego is notified thanks to his tech of the arrival of the Zonia Empire soldiers and gears up, he has a slim mech suit with a miniaturized quantum electric generator in a tube attached to the back, the chest and the palms of the hands having an energy blaster in the center and a jetpack / backpack attachment that can store weaponry and allow him flight.

After about an hour of setup Gego flies towards the Zonia Empire soldiers but is quickly bested as they strategically shoot his jetpack, making him crash-land while also destroying multiple weapons that were housed in the jetpack, Gego attempts to battle on anyway, shooting blasts from his hands that get deflected by the Zonia Empire soldiers with new tech. Gego is attacked from behind by cloaked soldiers that stomp on him as soon as he falls over from being whacked in the head with a pipe. Gego deploys a smoke bomb and escapes through a secret tunnel he created.

After recovery, Gego gets straight to work upgrading his mech suit, the Zonia Empire soldiers are sticking around so he can't add anything advanced because they'll destroy the entire planet to find Gego, he has to fix the durability again while repairing his arsenal, Gego does work on increasing the power output of the quantum electric generator double-fold, Gego then prepares once more for battle in his upgraded mech armor, the mark 13 armor.

Gego returns to the battlefield ready, dodging or blocking every shot the soldiers take, he pulls out a handle from his jetpack that at the click of a button transforms into a sword that goes from a dull gray metal to a glowing blue, Gego lands finally, in the center of a circle of Zonia Empire soldiers, his sword now back to the dull gray, they all shoot at Gego but either the armor is barely effected or Gego dodges, eventually Gego grabs the hilt of the sword with both hands and then it begins to glow once more as he slices the heads off the Zonia Empire soldiers, victory.
