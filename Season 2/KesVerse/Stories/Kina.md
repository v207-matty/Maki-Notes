In the Teinom realm there exists an organization that watches over the galaxies contained within the realm known as the Gixaka Watch, supported by the Anile themselves this group ensures that the realm is safe from any threat, external or internal.

Working for the Gixaka Watch there exists a regular mortal who was once powerless, but as they trained and trained, becoming stronger and stronger every day, they started to train their body to posses powers, they just needed to manipulate their energy to a precise degree, this mortal, Kina, uses their strength and ability to help the Gixaka Watch protect the galaxies.

One day however, Kina notices something, The Zonia Empire, the most fierce mortal empire in the realm is getting closer and closer to an artifact known as the Kesumel, with this artifact the head of the empire, Zonia himself would become unstoppable, they cannot let this happen, so Kina reports this to the higher ups, they say they will begin procedures to prevent it but days pass and nothing happens, strange, this is very similar to when Kina was reporting other bad actors and nothing happened, but they were minor disturbances and got sorted out anyway, but this is The Zonia Empire, you don't take this empire lightly, Kina begins looking into this, looking at confidential documents and records, listening in to discussions from the higher ups, nothing unusual at first but then Kina finds a document
