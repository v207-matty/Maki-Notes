# Kina : The Second Protector

In the vacuum of space, Kina drives their escape pod towards planet Burvak, there is a target of the Zonia Empire there that Kina wants to see, if they're not dead.

Kina arrives on the planet Burvak, they exit it and notice several miles of destruction, if this planet had a hero on it, they're most likely dead, but suddenly Kina is attacked by a someone in a mech suit, Kina has trouble avoiding their attacks but slowly they start to calm and sense the energy of the attacker, reading their intent before they can act on it, it's difficult to do but Kina manages and dodges most of their attacks, then they begin shooting at Kina, so Kina decides to charge and fire an energy blast at the center of the mechs chest and at the palms where the blasters are, the mech begins to try and run away but Kina catches up and stops them, pushing them to the floor. "Who are you and why did you attack?", the attacker takes the head of the mech off, revealing a humanoid figure, "My...my name is Gego! I...I thought you were with Zonia. Sorry.", Kina grabs Gegos hand and lifts him back up so he can stand on his feet.

"I ain't with Zonia! I'm tryna go against him!", "If you're interested in joining me you're welcome to.", Kina looks over at their pod, then behind it, noticing a Zonia Empire ship, "I think we have the perfect transportation means anyway if we can get that working.", Gego looks at the ship, "Oh yeah I can get that working, I used to be apart of the Zonia Empire, and I doubt they changed their infrastructure that much.", Kina looks at Gego confusingly, then chuckles. "All right then Gego, let's go do this!"

Kina and Gego both get in the Zonia Empire ship and head to the already inputted coordinates, going who knows where.

Through the vacuum of space, Kina and Gego fly through, going where-ever their stolen Zonia Empire ship is taking them, in the mean time Kina has been training their Nei control, and Gego has been using whatever he can on the ship to fix his armor or to at least provide a substitute until this mission is complete, Kina grows and Gego works, as they are approaching a planet they notice another Zonia Empire ship going the same direction except slower, Gego notices and gets slightly worried, they're descending onto the planet when suddenly they are attacked by a giant ground pillar that shoots through the ship and then retracts, taking Gegos helmet with it, as the ship falls onto the planet, Kina notices a figure flying in the air with their palm pointed at the ground, then they crash onto the planet. Kina looks at the dashboard of the ship, "Planet : Musele", "Species : Keselins", "Danger Level : Medium", "Calculated Planet Average Nei : 168.65".

Kina and Gego crawl out of the crashed ship and are met by the entire population, staring down at them, as they fully leave the ship, hands in the air, the population erupts in rage, moving towards Kina and Gego, there are demands for the murder of Kina and Gego, accusations that the Zonia Empire knows of the prophecy and want to stop it, then a giant ice and rock wall forms separating the population from Kina and Gego, comes down is the same figure Kina saw earlier, they speak, "Who are you?", "Why are you here?", "Are you with Zonia?", "What do you know about the Kesumel?". Gego asks a question of his own, "Who are you?", the person replies "Kumel.", Kina begins to answer Kumels questions, "Hi Kumel... I am Kina and this is Gego, we're here because we took a Zonia Empire ship to try and find Zonias main ship, we are not with Zonia, we are against him...", "The only thing I know about the Kesumel is that with it, Zonia will become a god. Nothing else."

Kumel walks around Gego and Kina, circling them multiple times slowly while speaking, "I could believe you, but I don't feel inclined to, for all I know this is some surprise attack from Zonia, I have a purpose to help my people.", Kumel grabs both Kina and Gego with the ground, "And I don't intend on taking risks.", Kumel makes the ground wrap around Kina and Gego, going from their feet to near their abdomen when another Zonia Empire ship lands beside them, Kumel ceases his murder attempt and walks over, Gego activates a mini blade in his mechs wrist, powers it up, and slices the ground so that Kina and him are freed, Kumel looks over noticing this and grunts.

The other Zonia Empire ship opens up, everyone freezes, looking over, and a single person comes out the ship, "Hey there!", he looks around, " Sorry to interrupt, uhm, my name is Stone, have any of you met a guy named 'Kuro'?", no answer, "Okay, uhm, do you guys know anyone? From this... Zonia Empire?", No answer but Gego looks away, Stone sighs in disappointment, moving back into the ship, "Okay then, I'll be off.", Kina screams out "WAIT!", everyone looks at them now, "It seems we've all got something going on with the Zonia Empire, why don't we all partner up?", Stone walks towards Kina, sticking out a hand, "I'm down, what's your name?", Kina shakes his hand, "Kina, and this over here is Gego and the other is Kumel.", Kumel looks frustrated and confused for a second, thinks about it, and calms down. "So now what?", Kina looks to the sky, "We find Zonia."
