# Zonia

On the west side of Chabur, a city is being destroyed by Tirok and his squads.
Stone flies into said city, attacking several soldiers all at once.
Stone flies further west.
The soldiers and Tirok follow him.

On the east side of Chabur, close to T-City, the forests are being searched and taken down by Nitro and his squads.
Sinco runs into one of the forests being taken down.
All the soldiers see him.
Sinco runs into the most dense forest on Chabur.
The soldiers and Nitro follow.

In T-City, Kumel is flying Kina, Donata, and Gego into the Zonia Empire Mothership.
As they enter the mothership Kumel freezes guards that see them and Donata shoves them so that the ice and them break.
Gego and Kina find the primary power room and go to deactivate it.
Gego pulls out some tools when Kumel freezes the power source.
Kumel grabs it and shatters it in their hand.

The mothership descends to the ground at an increasing pace.
The mothership crashes into T-city, a massive explosion resulting from it causing further damage.

Sinco is running towards T-City from the west.
Stone is flying towards T-City from the east.
Tirok and his squads are following Stone.
Nitro and his squads are following Sinco.

They all meet in the middle, Stone continuing to fly west.
