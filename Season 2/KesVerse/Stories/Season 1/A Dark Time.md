# A Dark Time

The Zonia Empire mothership releases several small Zonia Empire ships.

Sinco and Donata meet in the forest, running into each other.
Sinco, mad, tries to fight Donata but she subdues Sinco, knocking him out, and carrying him.

Donata wanders the forest, running into Gego, who was patrolling.
Donata pleads for help and Gego brings her over to The Protectors Zonia Empire ship.
The ship is hidden via it floating above the ocean and cloaking itself

Inside The Protectors Zonia Empire ship.
Sinco wakes up and Donata puts him down
Everyone is acquainted.
They need a plan.

Kina, Sinco, and Stone have no idea.
Kumel suggests stealing the Kesumel and give it to them.

Everyone agrees with that.

Nitro has a squad of soldiers looking for Sinco and Donata in the forest.

Inside The Protectors Zonia Empire ship.
They have a plan.

Sinco distracts Nitro and his squads.
Stone distracts Tirok and his squads.
Kumel brings Kina, Donata, and Gego into the ship.
Kumel and Donata will distract any guards or soldiers.

Everyone will meet up back on the ground, and they will deal with Tirok, Nitro, and Zonia, together.
