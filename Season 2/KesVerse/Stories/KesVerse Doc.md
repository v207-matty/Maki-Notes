# The Strong One

The planet Chacuric is a primitive society that consists of stone tools, houses with the bare minimum of structural integrity, the natives are reliant on farm land and water to survive, one day, several years ago came a pod with a child inside, this child is The Strong One, the child who as he grew, discovered his natural born strength and power, using these differences of his to help the world, becoming the primary protector of Chacuric once he ends The Menya War, where several countries were overtaken by Menya, but The Strong Ones interference has deemed the entire planet safe thanks to him, and that declaration can lead to bad actors seeking to disprove it or to use it.

One day a spy camera is discovered by The Strong One, and with its discover comes a warning of an arrival, once the warning is spoken the camera explodes, causing The Strong One to get hurt, this is a first for The Strong One, nothing had ever hurt him before, everyone on this planet could be handled by him with a finger, and yet this random piece of technology could harm him.

The next day a person arrived on Chacuric, they spoke of being a Zonia Empire Soldier here to deal with a potential threat and to colonize, The Strong One began battle with the soldier, giving every punch he lands his full potential, but the soldier stands there, eventually the soldier flicks The Strong One away, The Strong One charges back in, this was a fluke, right? The Strong One tackles the soldier, they both fall backward and The Strong One begins another flurry of punching, but still the soldier doesn't budge, the soldier raises up a hand and so does The Strong One, their hands both glow, The Strong One fires first and the soldier fires afterward, The Strong One is knocked back by this, falling onto his back on the ground, it feels as though with more power in the blast the soldier would've put a hole in his head, the soldier is unfazed however, getting up, walking towards The Strong One, and stomping on him, The Strong One cannot get up, he is severely weak from the face blast, The Strong One tries something anyway, he raises his hand, it gets grabbed by the soldier, the soldier gets off The Strong One and begins to drag him away to a ravine, right as the soldier is about to throw The Strong One in The Strong One has regained enough energy to quickly fly behind the soldier and push him into the ravine, killing him.

The Strong One : "I was... weak..."

That night The Strong One began to have nightmares where more of those soldiers came and that The Strong One couldn't do anything about it, they would take over Chacuric with no effort, the following day The Strong One looks to resolve this worries by helping more, he strong enough and powerful enough to help, yesterday was a fluke from the peaceful years they've experienced.

Two days later more Zonia Empire Soldiers arrive on Chacuric, but instead of flying towards them to beat them The Strong One freezes up and cowers in a secret hiding spot of his from when he was younger, the soldiers cause destruction across the planet for an entire day, then deciding to proceed with colonization procedures and leaving to get a bigger ship, The Strong One is too disappointed in himself to go back, he flies upward, hoping that the vacuum of space can free him from his worries, but it turns out he can breathe in space, so The Strong One flies to the moon and sits down in disappointment.

The Strong One spends a quarter of the day on the moon, thinking, but now a major thought has finally, why not get stronger? The issue was that the soldiers were stronger than him, getting stronger is the logical next step, nothing is stopping him, and so The Strong One returns to Chacuric, flying as fast as he can back to the ground, despite his hard landing, the planet is fine, its like a boulder fell to the ground, only the ground and or people close by can feel it, no matter, that's irrelevant right now, The Strong One needs to train to stay The Strong One, and so he does, for the rest of the day he trains as hard as he can, discovering that he was strong relative to the natives of the planet and that the planet itself was stronger than he, but with the massive power gap The Strong One could catch up faster once he started putting the effort in, this continues for half of the next day, but there is the arrival of a ship in the center of Chacuric.

A ship from The Zonia Empire has arrived on Chacuric, ready to colonize it as official Zonia Empire property, 8 Zonia Empire soldiers come out, 2 stand guard while the rest begin the colonization process, the Chacuric people ready to be captured and most likely enslaved when suddenly something flies cleanly through the ship, cutting it in half and causing an explosion that kills the 2 guard Zonia Empire soldiers. Above everyone flies The Strong One, ready to handle this, the Chacuric residents cheer as The Strong One handles the Zonia Empire soldiers, 4 of them went down easy but the other 2 came with challenge, The Strong One weakens them first by not letting them get any hit in or leverage, constant pressure and disorientation thanks to The Strong Ones speed, then when he is done The Strong One pushes one of the soldiers towards the other and fires an energy blast, taking out the last 2.

The citizens of Chacuric are ecstatic, cheering at the top of their lungs : "The Strong One!", but then The Strong One tells them to stop and to call him "Stone", and then everyone cheers "Stone The Strong One!".

All is well, for now.


# Sinco

On the planet Chabur, inside a giant forest late in the day, Sinco jumps from tree to tree, at incredible speeds, his hands grabbing branches as they come close, his tail grabbing branches below and then swinging off it to the next branch, Sincos tail then begins to twitch right before the next branch Sinco grabs snaps off the tree, Sinco instantly grabbed another branch and held onto the one that snapped, Sinco swings lower down, waiting. Several animals walk by in groups or by themselves, fast animals, slow animals, large animals, small animals, several animals, then 2 slow and small but meaty animals walk by, and Sinco pounces right at them, stabbing one and grabbing the other with his tail and then grabbing the tail and holding it in the air, then once Sinco ensures the first animal is dead he stabs the other animal, straight through the heart, dropping the corpses Sinco raises his right hand up at a tree, shifting his hand to point towards the ground behind it, then his hand glows for a second and then a beam of energy comes out, piercing the ground, Sinco quickly swipes his hand to the ground on the right of the tree and stops his beam, cutting the tree and causing it to collapse, Sinco then beams the tree to create a couple of logs, Sinco grabs the logs, stabs both the animals on the same branch, and walks towards the edge of the forest, right by the ocean, setting up a campfire, chopping up the animals into bits, and warming them up with the fire.

Sincos tail then begins to twitch rapidly, trees start falling inside the forest, and a chain reaction of trees collapsing on one another causes Sinco to nearly get crushed by a giant tree, Sincos campfire and food are crushed, and Sinco is mad, Sinco runs into the forest, arriving at the near center in 8 seconds, but is interrupted by several machines cutting down the tree with the only notable detail being giant letters on the sides : "TAZ", there is smaller text but Sinco can't make it out before having to jump up onto the trees, but they're chopped down as they are in the machines way, Sinco jumps towards trees to the side, Sinco grabs some branches and jumps at the machines to try and stab them, the branches break, Sinco jumps to the ground behind the machines and grabs some loose logs that didn't get collected by the machines, Sinco jumps again at the machines and the logs only dent the machines, Sinco then shoots a point-blank energy blast at one of the machines, it explodes, Sinco is knocked down to the ground, as he is getting up his tail begins to twitch rapidly and then he's grabbed by his tail by a flying ship with a claw arm.

A surge of pain goes through Sincos entire body, he can't do anything, the flying ship has the same "TAZ" label but Sinco can see more, "Tirok And Zon...", Sinco can't read the rest as he gets moved in front of the ship, the front opens up and reveals a man with a black trench coat, pants, hair and sclera with red eyes, who introduces himself as "Tirok". "You dirty Chabeten need to stay out of my way", Sinco get furious, "DO NOT CALL ME A CHABIN!", Sincos hands glow and are forced in front of Tirok, an energy beam being fired with an explosion coming from it that knocks back Sinco but has no effect on Tirok besides damaged clothes, Tirok grabs Sinco and brings Sincos face closer to his. "Try and stop me". Sinco gets tossed backwards by Tirok and then to the ground by the ship as Tirok goes back into the ship, as Sinco hits the ground his consciousness snaps, hours have passed and it is now the middle of the night, the moon at the top of the sky. The forest now has a clear pathway in the center of it from the city to the ocean, not a single tree in the way of it. Sinco gets up, looks behind him, and runs toward a giant factory with the same big letters : "TAZ".

In 4 seconds Sinco has arrived and enters the TAZ factory, he is disgusted by the machines he sees, many machines are there, their count never below the double digits, machines for making wood into paper, machines for clothing creation, machines for research, machines for power generation, machines for experiments and tests, machines for food production, machines for machine creation, machines for skin manufacturing, machine for artificial intelligence creation, machines for researching compatibility of machine and man, machines for packaging and shipping, it disgusts Sinco, he notices an office at the end of the massive building that in length would be half of Sinco's home forest if it only went on in one direction, Sinco gets into an idle stance, Sinco takes a breath, Sinco rubs his hands together, he makes his hands generate energy, he winds up, and Sinco RUNS to the end of the building, his hands generating and firing energy blasts that destroys the walls, the machines, the rooms, the wiring, everything, in 6 seconds flat Sinco is already at the end, his tail twitches and at near the same time a blast hits a power generator and a massive explosion emerges right as Sinco opens the office door, both Sinco and Tirok fly out of the building, machines inside exploding one after another creating more and more openings to the outside, making a bigger and bigger explosion that begins to spread into the city as the power grids begin to explode and debris flies EVERYWHERE.

Sincos tail twitches rapidly, he tries to move but is kicked hard, which kicks in his adrenaline, making him jump upward onto a lamp post. "YOU STUPID, IDIOT, CHABIN." is screamed by Tirok, his skin melting to reveal robotics and his clothes melting. "I told you... not to call me that..." Sinco says, his body in pain, Sincos tail twitches, Tiroks feet turn into thrusters as he flies at Sinco attempting to grab him, Sinco dodges by wrapping his tail around the lamp post so he can swing around it and bump into Tirok to push him back but it fails and Sinco is hurt by the exchange, Tirok grabs his tail, yanking it, forcing Sinco to swing back and be dangled in the air, face in front of Tiroks as Tiroks arm extends to allow them to be at eye level. "Try. And. Stop. Me." As Sincos tail tries to twitch and begins to go numb rapidly, Tirok tosses Sinco into a building, flying immediately at him as they both crash through the building, Tirok grabs onto Sinco and performing the final thrust to send them both out of the building, tossing Sinco to the ground as they exit the building from the other side. Sinco is about to fall straight into the ground but is caught by the tail by Tirok who tosses Sinco into the sky, pointing his other hand's index and middle fingers at Sinco, firing an energy blast that hits Sinco right before he begins to fall, Tirok then flies at Sinco at incredible speeds, punching Sinco as hard as he can so he doesn't rip a hole in Sincos chest, then grabbing Sinco by the chest, getting eye level with him again. "If you ever get in my way again, if you ever break my shit again, it's not going to be only me after your ass, I've got a partner, and once he is done with his thing, which I KNOW he will be done soon, you're dead, your forest, dead, no, NO! You won't be dead, you'll be a slave to his empire, he does love his slaves. I promise you, he will NOT, go easy on you, he has no reason to." Tirok then tosses Sinco towards his forest, overshooting so that Sinco falls into the ocean, but as Sinco passes the forest, getting low enough, he grabs onto a tree, and finally his body can stop accelerating, and he can rest, slowly getting up to stand on top of the tree, his legs wobbling and his tail flaccid. "I won't give him a reason."
