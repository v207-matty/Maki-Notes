## Example

Base: River

Basic: nam
- n : river
- v : to go, come

Place: nama / anama
- n : floodplain, river valley
- v : to water crops

Animal: namai / naim
- n : fish
- n (colloquial) : anything that lives in the water
- v : to fish

Collective: namo
- n : network of rivers, river system
- n (colloquial) : delta, tributary confluence
- n : traffic (of people, goods and transporation)
- n : trade (as a movement/exchange of goods; exchange and communication of ideas)
- v  

Abstract: nami
- n : life-river (world-view; to live life like the river)
- n : the irreversible passage of time
