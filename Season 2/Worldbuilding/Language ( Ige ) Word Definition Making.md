## Example

Base: River

Basic: nam
- n : river
- v : to go, come

Place: nama / anama
- n : floodplain, river valley
- v : to water crops

Animal: namai / naim
- n : fish
- n (colloquial) : anything that lives in the water
- v : to fish
- v : to prepare fish for market
- v : to set up a stall at market

Collective: namo
- n : network of rivers, river system
- n (colloquial) : delta, tributary confluence
- n : traffic (of people, goods and transportation)
- n : trade (as a movement/exchange of goods; exchange and communication of ideas)
- v : to flow (of liquids, objects and people)
- v : to pass (of people and time)
- v : to exchange (news, goods, ideas, money)

Abstract: nami
- n : life-river (world-view; to live life like the river)
- n : the irreversible passage of time
- v : to behave or act as the river, to follow the life-river

Adjective: namir
- riverine, river-like of or relating to river
- flowing
- passing, short-lived, fleeting
- profitable, lucrative, fruitful
- traffic congested, crowded with people
- busy, occupied with something
- fishy, one who is like a fish, hard to pin down, agile, nimble, elusive


## Chabur

Chabur (Singular) / Chabet (Plural) : Positive connotation
- N: One (or multiple) that is / are above others
- V: To be above others in status

Chabui (Singular) : Positive connotation
- N: An extraordinary thing
- V: To be above all in status

Chabin : Negative connotation
- N: A weird or unusual thing
- V: To be different
