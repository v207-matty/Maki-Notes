# Plot

## Exposition

- Environment is a forest on the planet Chabur
	- It is late
- Sinco jumps from tree to tree at incredible speeds
	- His hands for branches above his head
	- His tail for branches below and so that he can swing for momentum
- Sincos tail twitches slightly
- A branch snaps once Sinco grabs it
	- Sinco is prepared and instantly grabbed onto another branch at the same time
	- Sinco keeps the branch that snapped
- Sinco swings to a tree below that is closer to the ground
	- Sinco waits on top of the tree
- Several animals walk by either by themselves or in a group
	- Sinco is waiting for the right moment to strike
- 2 animals walk below
	- The animals are slow and easier to capture
- Sinco pounces at the animals
	- He stabs one of them and then stomps on the others tail so that it wont escape
	- Sinco then stabs the other one
- Sinco raises a hand toward the closest tree to him
	- Out comes a beam of glowing energy that punctures the tree
	- Sinco moves his hand so that the tree gets fully cut
	- The tree collapses
- Sinco cuts chunks out of the tree, creating tiny logs
	- Sinco grabs 10 tiny logs and the animal corpses
	- Sinco wanders off
- Sinco is now at an edge of the forest
	- The ocean sits right beside him
	- He has a fire setup
	- He has the animal corpses chopped to bits
	- He has 1 stick that he uses to pierce the animal corpse bits, dangle them over the fire, and eat them
- Sincos tail twitches rapidly
- Trees begin falling inside the forest
- A chain reaction of trees falling nearly gets Sinco killed
	- The last tree in the chain falls in front of him on top of his campfire
- Sinco runs inside the forest
	- Sinco makes it to the center of the massive forest in 8 seconds with just running
	- Several machines with a "TAZ" label are cutting down trees
- Sinco jumps upward on top of a tree and swings upward
- Sinco grabs 2 branches and pounces at one of the machines
	- Sinco jabs the branches into the machines shell as hard as he can and the sticks break
- Sinco jumps back upward
	- Sinco lazers out 2 logs that will fit inside his hands
- Sinco pounces on another of the machines
	- Sinco jabs the logs into the machines shell as hard as he can and it doesn't do anything
- Sinco jumps back up onto the tree
- Sinco raises his hand
- Sinco fires several energy blasts at the machines that leave noticeable dents or holes
	- The machines start to slow
- Sincos tail twitches so fast that its almost imperceptible
	- Sinco begins to turn around as he jumps off the tree

## Rising Action

- Sinco is grabbed mid air by the tail
	- A surge of pain goes through Sincos entire body
	- Sinco struggles to do anything
- Sinco is being held by the tail by a flying mechanical ship with an arm popping out of the bottom and the same "TAZ" label as the machines
- The ship opens from the top and out comes a regular human with black hair, a closed black trench coat and pants
	- The man introduces himself as "Tirok"
- Tirok asks if Sinco is one of the "Dirty Chabin" that keeps messing with his machines
	- Sinco denies this : "I ain't no Chabin! I am a Chabui! You're the Chabin! You're destroying my home!"
	- Tirok: "Your weird self and your home is in the way of the future, either get with the program or get lost."
- Sincos tail goes numb
- Tirok enters the ship
	- The ship lets go of Sinco
	- The ship retracts the arm into its bottom
- Sinco grabs onto a tree branch with his hand and swings towards the ship, landing on top of it
	- Sinco: "My parents always told me you regulars were assholes."
	- Sinco raises his free hand to charge an energy blast
- Sincos tail suddenly sends a wave of pain throughout Sincos body as it returns from numbness
- Tiroks ship releases another arm from the behind that grabs Sinco
- Sinco gets tossed to the ground with no trees to help him
- Sinco hits the ground
	- The full impact of the toss and fall spreads throughout his entire body
- Tirok speaks before leaving : "If you Chabeten weren't assholes or jerks most of the time with your differences, maybe WE wouldn't be assholes!"
	- Tirok flies away to the sky in his ship, watching the deforestation
- Sinco falls unconscious
- Sinco wakes up, hours later, mid day
- The forest has a clear pathway in the center of it from the city to the ocean, not a single tree in the way of it
- Sinco gets up
	- He can feel his tail again
	- He's no longer in pain
- Sinco looks toward the city and sees a factory with the "TAZ" label
	- Sinco runs towards the factory

## Climax

- Sinco enters the TAZ factory
	- Several machines are there
		- Turning wood into sheets of paper
		- Developing and managing Artificial Intelligences
		- Creating Clothing in rapid pace
		- Doing research on animal populations, reproduction rates, and more
		- Creating food from animal remains
		- Managing packaging and shipping for stores
		- Generating Power
		- Performing experiments and tests
		- Manufacturing fake skin
		- Manufacturing Robotic bodies
- Sinco looks around and is disgusted
- Sinco notices an office
- Sinco raises his hands
- Sinco shoots several energy blasts at different machines
	- Several explosions emerge from system failings and from collision with the energy blasts
- Sinco jumps around from machine to machine, destroying nearly every machine around him
- Sinco nearly arrives at the office, shooting one more energy blast before he arrives
- Sincos tail twitches rapidly
- The energy blast hits one of the Power Generators and a massive explosion chain reaction begins
	- Sinco is hit with the explosion and goes through several walls of concrete

## Falling Action

- Sinco lays on the concrete of outside
	- Sinco lays on the road
	- Several explosions happen one after another in the city

- Sincos tail twitches rapidly
	- Sinco tries to move
- Sinco gets kicked
	- Sincos adrenaline kicks in
- Sinco jumps up
	- Sinco grabs a lamp post with his tail and spins to land on it
- Tirok stands below, furious, his skin melting to reveal robotics, his clothing melting
	- Tirok: "YOU, STUPID, IDIOT, CHABIN."

- Sincos tail twitches
- Tirok jumps at Sinco
	- Sinco jumps off the lamppost
	- Tirok lands on the lamppost
	- Sinco spins around the lamppost with his tail
	- Sinco kicks Tirok
- One of Tiroks hands turn into a rocket and Tirok shoots upward, launching upward
	- Tiroks feet turn into thrusters and he recomposes
- Sinco pounces at Tirok
	- Sinco grabs onto his head
- Sinco bangs and scratches on Tirok

- Sincos tail twitches
- Sinco dangles from Tiroks leg thanks to his tail
- Tirok swings his right hand

- Sincos tail twitches
- Sinco swings upward to punch Tirok in the head
	- It hurts and stuns Sinco

- Sincos tail twitches
- Tirok swings his right hand back and hits Sinco straight in the face
- Tirok spins around
- Tirok stops spinning suddenly
	- Inertia makes Sinco fly off Tiroks leg

## Resolution

Tirok ends up victorious
