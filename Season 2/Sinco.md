# Plot

## Exposition

- Environment is a forest on the planet Chabur
- Sinco jumps from tree to tree at incredible speeds
	- His hands for branches above his head
	- His tail for branches below and so that he can swing for momentum
- Sincos tail twitches slightly
- A branch snaps once Sinco grabs it
	- Sinco is prepared and instantly grabbed onto another branch at the same time
	- Sinco keeps the branch that snapped
- Sinco swings to a tree below that is closer to the ground
	- Sinco waits on top of the tree
- Several animals walk by either by themselves or in a group
	- Sinco is waiting for the right moment to strike
- 2 animals walk below
	- The animals are slow and easier to capture
- Sinco pounces at the animals
	- He stabs one of them and then stomps on the others tail so that it wont escape
	- Sinco then stabs the other one
- Sinco raises a hand toward the closest tree to him
	- Out comes a beam of glowing energy that punctures the tree
	- Sinco moves his hand so that the tree gets fully cut
	- The tree collapses
- Sinco cuts chunks out of the tree, creating tiny logs
	- Sinco grabs 10 tiny logs and the animal corpses
	- Sinco wanders off
