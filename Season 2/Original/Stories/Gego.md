On the planet of Burvak there exists a man named "Gego" with the most advanced technology ever created on said planet, and yet he keeps it all to himself, this is so that he may be prepared for the arrival of more soldiers from The Zonia Empire on his planet, he was once apart of their ranks but due to a dispute there was a falling out and Gego was at the time very impulsive, causing major sabotages to The Zonia Empire systems, making them fall behind schedule by 5 years.

That day has arrived, 8 Zonia Empire soldiers arrive on Burvak looking for Gego, causing destruction every where they go, sending the general population back technology wise by entire decades. Gego is notified thanks to his tech of the arrival of the Zonia Empire soldiers and gears up, he has a slim mech suit with a miniaturized quantum electric generator in the back, the chest and the palms of the hands having an energy blaster in the center and a jetpack / backpack attachment that can store weaponry and allow him flight.

After about an hour of setup Gego flies towards the Zonia Empire soldiers but is quickly bested as they strategically shoot his jetpack, making him crash-land while also destroying multiple weapons that were housed in the jetpack, Gego attempts to battle on anyway

Gego is defeated by the Zonia Empire soldiers and retreats

Gego upgrades his tech, fixing the weak points that were exploited in the last fight

Gego returns to deal with the Zonia Empire soldiers and ends up victorious
