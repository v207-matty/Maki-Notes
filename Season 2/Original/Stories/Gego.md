On the planet of Burvak there exists a man named "Gego" with the most advanced technology ever created on said planet, and yet he keeps it all to himself, this is so that he may be prepared for the arrival of more soldiers from The Zonia Empire on his planet, he was once apart of their ranks but due to a dispute there was a falling out and Gego was at the time very impulsive, causing major sabotages to The Zonia Empire systems, making them fall behind schedule by 5 years, and that was 13 years ago

That day has arrived, 8 Zonia Empire soldiers arrive on Burvak looking for Gego, causing destruction every where they go, sending the general population back technology wise by entire decades. Gego is notified thanks to his tech of the arrival of the Zonia Empire soldiers and gears up, he has a slim mech suit with a miniaturized quantum electric generator in a tube attached to the back, the chest and the palms of the hands having an energy blaster in the center and a jetpack / backpack attachment that can store weaponry and allow him flight.

After about an hour of setup Gego flies towards the Zonia Empire soldiers but is quickly bested as they strategically shoot his jetpack, making him crash-land while also destroying multiple weapons that were housed in the jetpack, Gego attempts to battle on anyway, shooting blasts from his hands that get deflected by the Zonia Empire soldiers with new tech. Gego is attacked from behind by cloaked soldiers that stomp on him as soon as he falls over from being whacked in the head with a pipe. Gego deploys a smoke bomb and escapes through a secret tunnel he created.

After recovery, Gego gets straight to work upgrading his mech suit, the Zonia Empire soldiers are sticking around so he can't add anything advanced because they'll destroy the entire planet to find Gego, he just has to fix the durability again while repairing his arsenal, Gego does work on increasing the power output of the quantum electric generator

Gego returns to deal with the Zonia Empire soldiers and ends up victorious
