## Exposition

Establish Gego : Regular with THE most advanced tech on his planet

On the planet of Burvak there exists a man named "Gego" with the most advanced technology ever created on said planet, and yet he keeps it all to himself, this is so that he may be prepared for the arrival of more so

## Rising Action

Zonia Empire soldiers arrive on Gegos planet to deal with him, causing destruction everywhere they go

## Climax

Gego is defeated by the Zonia Empire soldiers and retreats

## Falling Action

Gego upgrades his tech, fixing the weak points that were exploited in the last fight

## Resolution

Gego returns to deal with the Zonia Empire soldiers and ends up victorious
