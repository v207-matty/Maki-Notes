The planet Chacuric is a primitive society that consists of stone tools, houses with the bare minimum of structural integrity, the natives are reliant on farm land and water to survive, one day, several years ago came a pod with a child inside, this child is The Strong One, the child who as he grew, discovered his natural born strength and power, using these differences of his to help the world, becoming the primary protector of Chacuric once he ends The Menya War, where several countries were overtaken by Menya, but The Strong Ones interference has deemed the entire planet safe thanks to him, and that declaration can lead to bad actors seeking to disprove it or to use it.

One day a spy camera is discovered by The Strong One, and with its discover comes a warning of an arrival, once the warning is spoken the camera explodes, causing The Strong One to get hurt, this is a first for The Strong One, nothing had ever hurt him before, everyone on this planet could be handled by him with a finger, and yet this random piece of technology could harm him.

The next day a person arrived on Chacuric, they spoke of being a Zonia Empire Soldier here to deal with a potential threat and to colonize, The Strong One began battle with the soldier, giving every punch he lands his full potential, but the soldier just stands there, eventually the soldier flicks The Strong One away, The Strong One charges back in, surely this was just a fluke, The Strong One tackles the soldier, they both fall backward and The Strong One begins another flurry of punching, but still the soldier doesn't budge, the soldier raises up a hand and so does The Strong One, their hands both glow, The Strong One fires first and the soldier fires afterward, The Strong One is knocked back extremely by this, falling onto his back on the ground, it feels as though with a bit more power in the blast the soldier would've put a hole in his head, the soldier is unfazed however, getting up, walking towards The Strong One, and stomping on him, The Strong One cannot get up, he is severely weak from the face blast, The Strong One tries something anyway, he raises his hand, it gets grabbed by the soldier, the soldier gets off The Strong One and begins to drag him away to a ravine, right as the soldier is about to throw The Strong One in The Strong One has regained enough energy to quickly fly behind the soldier and push him into the ravine, killing him.

The Strong One : "I was... weak..."

That night The Strong One began to have nightmares where more of those soldiers came and that The Strong One couldn't do anything about it, they would take over Chacuric with no effort, the following day The Strong One looks to resolve this worries by helping more, he strong enough and powerful enough to help, yesterday was just a fluke from the peaceful years they've experienced.

Two days later more Zonia Empire Soldiers arrive on Chacuric, but instead of flying towards them to beat them The Strong One freezes up and cowers in a secret hiding spot of his from when he was younger, the soldiers cause destruction across the planet for an entire day, then deciding to proceed with colonization procedures and leaving to get a bigger ship, The Strong One is too disappointed in himself to go back, he just flies upward, hoping that the vacuum of space can free him from his worries, but it turns out he can breathe in space, so The Strong One just flies to the moon and sits down in disappointment.

The Strong One spends a quarter of the day on the moon, just thinking, but now a major thought has finally, why not just get stronger? The issue was that the soldiers were stronger then him, getting stronger is the logical next step, nothing is stopping him, and so The Strong One returns to Chacuric, flying as fast as he can back to the ground, despite his hard landing, the planet is fine, its just like a boulder fell to the ground, only the ground and or people close by can feel it, no matter, that's irrelevant right now, The Strong One needs to train to stay The Strong One, and so he does, for the rest of the day he trains as hard as he can, discovering that he was just strong relative to the natives of the planet and that the planet itself was stronger then he, but with the massive power gap The Strong One could catch up faster once he just started putting the effort in, this continues for half of the next day, but there is the arrival of a ship in the center of Chacuric.
