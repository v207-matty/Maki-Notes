## Initial Concept

A being with immense power on a foreign planet that is raised on said planet and learns of its power and uses it to be a good person on that planet, with its brothers and sisters eventually finding the planet, working for a higher power and having to do horrible things to the planets life and the being that was raised there which now uses its powers to stop its brothers and sisters and to stop the higher powers that caused their rampage on the beings new home.
