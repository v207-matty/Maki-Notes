Also known as "The Blessing"

In the late 19th century, during [The American Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War).

[[Earth]] was visited by [[Tosullins]] from [[Tosmut]] and granted "[[The Tosullin Grain]]".

[The Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_(American_Civil_War)) were told to point it at the atmosphere,
and they would be able to achieve amazing abilities
once they felt intent to send them out.

[The Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_(American_Civil_War)) listened, a beam was released,
and the earth's atmosphere was fueled with magical capabilities,
thanks to [[The Tosullin Grain]], spreading across the world,
slowly allowing the capability of magic to spread to everyone.

And as the [[Tosullins]] left, [The Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_(American_Civil_War)) were confused as nothing really happened besides the beam...

But something will happen, just slowly.
