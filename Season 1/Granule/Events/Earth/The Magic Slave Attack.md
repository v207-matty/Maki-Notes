[[Earth]]

April 1865 - July 1865

When it was around the time [The Confederacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_States_of_America) would normally have surrendered in the real world, there was actually an attack on them by civilians of The South who had gained unnatural abilities, specifically resentful ex-slaves, which made many states from [The Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_(American_Civil_War)) move to [The Confederacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_States_of_America).

[The Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_(American_Civil_War)) didn't have enough states on it's side to be able to combat the [The Confederacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_States_of_America), and surrendered, and the 14th amendment was ratified, not long afterward, undoing the abolishment of slavery and involuntary servitude, causing [Abraham Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Lincoln) to step down from being the president, letting him live his life till old age.
