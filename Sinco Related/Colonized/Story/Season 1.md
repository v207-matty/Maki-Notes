## Season 1 Episode 1

August 8th, 2015

On July 19th, 2015, in a laboratory owned by Tirok Industries, a speedster humanoid was created with chaos energy, his name is Sinco.

And he is the most evil creature created on the Earth at that time.

Days and days of speeding around the sealed Tempo City, destroying buildings and harming anyone in their way.

Nothing can be done about him, he's too fast.

A new Tirok Industries project was finally funded and a new speedster was created to combat Sinco, and his name is Osin.

Today, Sinco and Osin meet and battle, destruction continues, but much more focused, they battle for hours, but then when Sinco was taken to be given a public execution, Osin stopped it, freed Sinco, and they both escape from the now unsealed Tempo City, causing destruction to the world.

## Season 1 Episode 2

October 17th, 2015

Over the last month and a few days, Tirok Industries has started hunting down Sinco and Osin for the third time since they were last seen.

Sinco and Osin had gone quiet days after their escape, not a single trail of destruction was found that showed evidence of them besides one time in the middle of September, making it much harder to find them, and today they finally found them, they're peacefully eating over a campfire

When the speedsters notice that they've been spotted, Osin surrenders while Sinco charges at them, attacking, Osin briefly wants to go after Sinco but sits back on the ground, Sinco is overpowered by the Tirok Industry forces not too soon after and both are arrested, but Sinco vibrates to a very high velocity that allows him to vibrate out of the cuffs and escape, Osin stays behind, disappointed in Sinco and is fully arrested by the T.I. (Tirok Industries) forces.

Back at T.I. in Tempo City, Osin, who has now been forgiven by the city with his explanation being that he hoped to change Sinco, works with Tirok to try and figure out ways to capture Sinco, but nothing can be done.

Osin says "As long as he doesn't do anything, he's fine. Right?"

Tirok agrees.

## Season 1 Episode 3

February 2nd, 2016

All is peaceful in Tempo City, no Sinco to be found, and T.I. have continued to provide amazing technological improvements to normal day society, they've finally mastered chaos energy and now it is slowly starting to be used on a global scale to power machines and to create new life in hopes for a new race of humans.

Today there was an incident however, inside of Tempo City begins a rebellion against the safety laws of chaos energy usage, claiming that people should be allowed to use the energy however they please since society found the chaos emeralds before the big corps ever did.

Osin was recruited by T.I. with part of their forces to handle the rebellion.

Osin seeks a peaceful resolution, but the rebellion members fire at them anyway, with the T.I. forces firing back, Osin uses his speed to incapacitate the rebellion members without harming them signifiganrly
